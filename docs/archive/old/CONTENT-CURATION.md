# Ethereum Gazette - Content Curation Guidelines

> Comprehensive policies and procedures for content source management and quality assurance

## Overview

Ethereum Gazette serves as a minimalist, curated front page of the Ethereum ecosystem. This document establishes the standards, policies, and procedures for content curation to ensure high-quality, relevant, and trustworthy information reaches our users.

## Core Principles

### 1. Ethereum-First Focus

- All content must be directly related to the Ethereum ecosystem
- Priority given to protocol-level developments, DeFi, L2s, and core infrastructure
- Adjacent topics (Bitcoin, other chains) only when directly relevant to Ethereum

### 2. Quality Over Quantity

- Credible sources with established track records
- Technical accuracy and factual reporting
- Editorial standards and professional presentation

### 3. Timeliness and Relevance

- Fresh content prioritized (last 7 days preferred)
- Breaking news and protocol updates given highest priority
- Evergreen educational content balanced with news

### 4. Diversity and Balance

- Multiple perspectives and viewpoints
- Geographic and demographic diversity in sources
- Balance across all content categories

## Content Standards

### Technical Accuracy

- Factual information verified against multiple sources
- Technical details checked for accuracy
- Protocol information verified against official documentation
- Mathematical and cryptographic claims validated

### Editorial Quality

- Clear, well-written content
- Proper attribution and sourcing
- Professional presentation
- Minimal grammatical errors

### Relevance Scoring

- **High (Priority):** Protocol changes, security issues, major launches
- **Medium:** Market analysis, educational content, community discussions
- **Low:** Opinion pieces, historical content, tangential topics

### Freshness Requirements

- **Breaking News:** Within 1 hour of publication
- **Daily Updates:** Within 24 hours
- **Weekly Content:** Within 7 days
- **Educational:** No strict time limits (evergreen content)

---

## Character Limits and Formatting

### Post Cards

- **Title:** 110 characters maximum
- **Snippet:** 270 characters maximum
- **HTML tags:** Stripped during processing
- **Encoding:** UTF-8 with HTML entity decoding

### Featured Content

- **Level 1 Title:** 100 characters maximum
- **Level 1 Description:** 200 characters maximum
- **Level 2 Title:** 50 characters maximum
- **Level 2 Description:** 100 characters maximum

### Optimization Guidelines

- Titles should be descriptive and actionable
- Snippets should provide clear value proposition
- Remove marketing language and hyperbole
- Preserve technical accuracy while improving readability

## Featured Content Curation

### Selection Criteria

1. **Immediate Impact:** Breaking news affecting Ethereum users
2. **Educational Value:** Content that improves ecosystem understanding
3. **Community Interest:** High engagement potential
4. **Strategic Importance:** Protocol developments and upgrades

### Featured Content Distribution

- **1 Level 1 Item:** Primary featured content (gradient background)
- **3 Level 2 Items:** Secondary featured content (standard cards)

### Update Frequency

- **Manual Admin Curation:** Featured content is manually selected by admin
- **Long-term Placement:** Items remain featured for extended periods (weeks/months)
- **Not Dynamic:** No automatic rotation or time-based scheduling
- **Strategic Updates:** Changes made for breaking news, major protocol updates, or strategic initiatives

### Content Management

- **Current:** Hardcoded in `app/src/data/featured.ts`
- **Planned:** Admin interface for featured content management
- **Approval Process:** Admin manually selects and places featured items
- **Version Control:** All changes tracked and deployable
- **Character Validation:** Automated validation for character limits
- Backup and rollback procedures
